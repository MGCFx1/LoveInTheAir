A letter for you

شهد، حبيبتي

Happy birthday, my love. Twenty-one years ago the world got you, and a little over a year ago, somehow, I did too.

I still think about how fast it happened. We met on the 13th of August, I asked for your hand five days later, and by the 29th you were my fiancée. People told us it was quick. It never felt quick to me. It felt like I had finally found something I had been looking for my whole life.

Then came the hardest part. Five months apart, then seven more. Screens instead of hands, time zones instead of dinners. You never once made me feel far away. You waited, you trusted, you kept choosing us, and every time I came back, you were exactly where I left you, smiling at me like no time had passed.

You are my calm, my home, and my favourite person to laugh with. I love the way you look at me in photos when you think I'm not looking. I love your patience, your heart, and how you make ordinary days feel like occasions.

The 29th has always been our number. It's the day we got engaged, and it's the day you came into this world. So on this 29th I want to promise you something: the distance is almost behind us, and everything we've waited for is ahead. I can't wait to build our life together.

Happy 21st, Shahd. I love you more than any website could ever hold.

Yours always, Mo
